About AirMate

AirMate is a GenAI-powered Urban Air Quality Assistant built for the PromptWars Virtual Challenge 06. It helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution with low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives - each explained with its direct impact.

Data & honesty

  • Air quality figures are model forecasts from the Copernicus CAMS global atmospheric composition model (~45 km resolution, updated twice daily), served by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0. They are estimates for your area, not readings from a ground sensor near you. For official Indian station readings, check CPCB's Sameer app.
  • AQI uses the US EPA AirNow scale and its health guidance bands (airnow.gov). India's national AQI bands differ slightly.
  • CO₂ impact figures are order-of-magnitude estimates from rounded UK Government GHG Conversion Factors 2026 averages. They compare choices; they are not measurements of your vehicle.
  • Health tips are general public guidance, not medical advice. Sensitive groups should follow their doctor's plan.

How the AI is used

The assistant chat, routine swaps, and indoor hacks are generated by a large language model through a small backend proxy. Your live air-quality context is sent with each request so answers stay grounded in today's conditions. No personal data is stored.
